Hard rules

  • Monday–Friday, 50-minute periods.
  • Theory: 1, 2, or 3 consecutive slots.
  • Lab: exactly 3 consecutive slots.
  • No teacher, section, room or lab overlap.
  • Unavailable faculty slots are never scheduled.
  • Locked/final entries are protected.
